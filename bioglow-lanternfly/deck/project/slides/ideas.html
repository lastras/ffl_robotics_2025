What we found

Six reasons people give

1 Boom and bust

Numbers shoot up for a few years, then drop on their own.

2 Predators caught on

Native animals learned that lanternflies are food.

3 Fungus diseases

Native fungi infect lanternflies and kill them.

4 Weather

A cold, snowy winter, a late frost, or a hot, dry summer.

5 People’s actions

Removing tree of heaven, squishing, and scraping eggs.

6 They moved on

Fewer here, but maybe more somewhere else.

Most experts in the stories we read said it is probably a mix, and that nobody knows for sure yet.
