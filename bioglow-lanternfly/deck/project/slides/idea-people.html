People’s actions

What people say

Years of removing tree of heaven, squishing, scraping eggs and trapping have added up.

A nature preserve near Philadelphia removed its tree of heaven: “I think that’s part of the reason we’ve seen less here.”

What we found

State guides say removing tree of heaven can greatly reduce lanternflies in an area. But they also feed on many other plants.

Egg masses are at all heights, so scraping only reaches some. We found no study showing that squishing lowers numbers across a region.

Our question for you

Which things that people do make a real difference, and which only feel helpful?
