Our first ideas for next year

Count every year

Walk the same route on the same dates each year and post photos to iNaturalist.

Winter egg hunt

Scrape the egg masses we can reach, from fall to spring. No ladders.

Map tree of heaven

Find where it grows in town and ask the owners about removing it.

Safer traps

Use circle traps when eggs hatch. Skip sticky bands, which can catch birds.

Protect the helpers

Spray less, so mantises, spiders and soldier bugs can do their job.

Don’t give them a ride

Check cars and outdoor gear for eggs and bugs before a trip.

Which of these would you do first? What is missing?

These are only first ideas. We will change them after we hear from the experts. Why each one: counting fixes the biggest gap we found, which is that nobody has real numbers. Egg scraping is safe for kids, but Penn State says fewer than 2 percent of egg masses are low enough to reach, so we should ask if it is worth the effort. Tree of heaven is the favorite host, and removing it needs an adult and usually a herbicide so it does not grow back. Penn State recommends circle traps from late April through June and does not recommend sticky bands. The Penn State predator researchers warned that insecticides also kill the predators. Checking cars slows the spread to new places. Ask the expert: which would you do first, and which would you drop?
