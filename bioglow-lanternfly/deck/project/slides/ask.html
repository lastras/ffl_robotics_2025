Interview questions

Decide before the meeting who asks each question and who writes down the answers. Always ask permission before recording. Good follow-up questions: “How do you know that?”, “Is there a study we can read?”, and “Who else should we talk to?” One more topic to raise if there is time: scientists are testing a tiny wasp from Asia, Dryinus sinicus, that attacks lanternfly nymphs. As of 2025 no biological control had been approved for release. Ask the expert whether that has changed.
