This idea fits with boom and bust. Places invaded early, like Philadelphia, are in the “bust” part. Places invaded later, like Washington DC, are still in the “boom” part. A parks manager in Arlington, Virginia expects a couple more years of build-up there before the numbers settle. So “fewer lanternflies” depends on where you live.
