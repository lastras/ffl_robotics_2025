The lanternfly in four numbers

2014

First found in the United States, in southeastern Pennsylvania. It came from Asia.

18+

States it had spread to by 2025. It travels as eggs stuck to cars, trains and firewood.

1

Generation each year. Adults die in the fall. Only the eggs live through winter.

30–50

Eggs in one egg mass. That is why numbers can grow so fast.

It drinks plant sap. Its favorite host is tree of heaven, which is also invasive. Vineyards worry about it the most.

Sources: Penn State News (2025), Popular Science (2024), WMGK (2026)
