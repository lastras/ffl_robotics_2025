Ratings by the team, based on the sources listed at the end

Explain how we rated: “strong” means scientists measured it over many years. “Medium” means there is a real study, but only in cages, or only in one place, or it does not directly explain a drop. “Weak” means people said it, but we could not find anyone who measured it. Weak does not mean wrong. It means we need an expert. The biggest gap we found is that nobody seems to count lanternflies the same way every year.
