Weather

What people say

The Philadelphia area had many snowstorms and a long cold stretch last winter.

In Virginia, an expert pointed to “the super late frost that knocks back insect populations” and “a really hot and dry summer.”

What we found

Eggs are tough. A Canadian government scientist said in 2026 that eggs can survive about −25 °C (−13 °F).

We found no study that measured how many eggs or nymphs died from last winter or the late frost.
