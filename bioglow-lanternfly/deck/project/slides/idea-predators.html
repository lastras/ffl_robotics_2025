Idea 2 of 6

Predators caught on

What people say

Native animals have learned that lanternflies are food.

A Virginia Tech insect expert said “spiders, lizards, other bugs are figuring out that they can eat these.”

What we found

Penn State tested 10 predator insects in cages. Spined soldier bugs and praying mantises ate the most.

Of almost 2,000 public reports of animals eating lanternflies, over half were insects and spiders, not birds.

Our question for you

Is there proof from outdoors that predators eat enough lanternflies to lower the numbers?

How could a team like ours measure that?

Sources: Penn State News (Mar 2025), Popular Science (2024), WHSV (Jul 2026)

The Penn State study was done in enclosures, which means cages. The researchers said the next step is to test outdoors, so we do not know yet how much predators matter in real life. They also warned that bug sprays can kill the helpful predators too. The Virginia Tech expert is Dr. Pat Wooden, lab manager of the Virginia Tech Insect Identification Laboratory.
